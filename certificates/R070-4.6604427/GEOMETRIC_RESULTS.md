# 同预算增强及其方法边界 / Same-budget enhancement and its scope limit

以下几何结果与原收费全域证书分别复验；原格式model.json是载体，overlay.json才定义增强规则。只把载体交给原检查器不能验证增强费用。 / The geometric results are replayed separately from the original-charge global certificate. The original-format model is a carrier; the overlay defines the enhanced rules. Passing only the carrier to the original checker does not validate the enhanced charge.

若资源的有限非空获胜站点集Wᵢ的凸包两两相交，则内部不交的开凸父至多一个触发该资源：父包含全部Wᵢ就包含conv(Wᵢ)，两父获胜将共享凸包交点，矛盾。因此每图像预算为1，获胜集无需共享同一站点。 / If the convex hulls of a resource's finite nonempty winning site sets Wᵢ intersect pairwise, at most one of any interior-disjoint open convex parents can win it: capturing Wᵢ places conv(Wᵢ) in the open parent, so two winners would share a hull-intersection point. Thus each image has budget one without requiring a shared site.

对每个轨道的全部有限站点子集S，精确核对f₀(S)≤f₁(S)，并核对获胜集非空性、最小性、凸包相交、完整D4图像及多重性、预算和整数安全界。保持非负权重得到F₁(P)≥F₀(P)、M₁=M₀，对所有父和核成立。 / For every finite site subset S of each orbit, verify f₀(S)≤f₁(S), nonempty minimal winning sets, hull intersections, complete D4 image multiplicities, budget and integer safety bounds. Retaining nonnegative weights gives F₁(P)≥F₀(P) and M₁=M₀ for all parents and cores.

公开CI还检查载体相对新全域证书只追加零权站点/规则、原有点位/规则/权重逐项相同，且预算相同。因此几何增强可继承原收费4.6604427结论；这不是声称增强费用另跑了一遍完整中心扫描，也不自动增大目标。 / Public CI also checks that the carrier only appends zero-weight sites/rules to the new global certificate, preserves all prior coordinates/rules/weights and retains the budget. ## 指定临界父的障碍 / Obstruction at a specified critical parent

假设直接点费和旧Boolean权重固定，保留所有旧充分触发条件，并继续用非空且两两相交的获胜凸包证明每图像预算1。允许旧资源内部增加任意有限新站点/获胜集。旧W仍触发意味着新家族中有R⊆W；若新集合S在父内触发，conv(S)位于父开内部而conv(R)⊆conv(W)与其分离，违反两两相交。故所有原未触发图像仍不触发，指定父费用无法提高。 / Assume fixed direct-site charges and old Boolean weights, preservation of every old sufficient trigger, and nonempty pairwise-intersecting winning hulls as the budget-one argument. Arbitrarily many finite new sites/winning sets may be added within old resources. Since old W still triggers, a new winner R⊆W exists; a newly captured winner S inside the parent would have conv(S) disjoint from conv(R)⊆conv(W), contradicting pairwise intersection. Thus no formerly non-firing image can fire, and this parent's charge cannot increase.

No seventeen-parent packing is constructed, so this is neither an upper bound on s(17) nor whole-dictionary infeasibility.
